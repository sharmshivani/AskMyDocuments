# Transfer-out policy (sample, fictional)

Members of the Contoso Pension Scheme may ask to transfer their benefits to another registered pension arrangement. A member must have at least three months of scheme membership before a transfer-out request can be accepted.

A transfer value quotation is valid for three months from the date it is issued. Each member receives one free quotation in any twelve-month period. Further quotations in the same period carry an administration fee of 150 pounds.

Where the transfer value is above 30,000 pounds, the member must provide evidence that they have received independent financial advice before the transfer can proceed. Members within twelve months of their normal retirement age must also complete an additional guidance session.

The scheme aims to complete a transfer within 60 days of receiving a complete application, including all discharge forms and identity checks. Incomplete applications pause the 60-day period until the missing information is supplied.
