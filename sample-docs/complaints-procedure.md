# Complaints procedure (sample, fictional)

Complaints about the Contoso Pension Scheme can be submitted by email or by post to the scheme administrator. Every complaint is logged with a reference number on the day it is received.

The administrator acknowledges each complaint within 5 working days. A full written response is provided within 30 working days of receipt. If more time is needed, the administrator writes to the complainant to explain the delay and gives a revised date.

If the complainant is not satisfied with the response, or has received no final response after 8 weeks, they may escalate the complaint to the Pensions Ombudsman. The administrator includes the Ombudsman's contact details in every final response letter.

Records of complaints, including correspondence and outcomes, are kept for six years. Complaints data is reviewed by the trustees every quarter to identify recurring problems.
