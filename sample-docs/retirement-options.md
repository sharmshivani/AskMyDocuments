# Retirement options (sample, fictional)

The normal retirement age of the Contoso Pension Scheme is 65. Members may choose to retire early from age 55. Early retirement reduces the pension by 4 percent for each full year that retirement is taken before normal retirement age.

Members who delay retirement beyond 65 receive an increase of 5 percent for each full year of delay, up to a maximum age of 70.

At retirement, a member may take up to 25 percent of the pension value as a tax-free lump sum. The remaining pension is paid as a regular income, which is taxable. Taking the maximum lump sum reduces the regular income accordingly.

If a member dies while still employed and contributing to the scheme, a death in service benefit of four times annual salary is paid to the nominated beneficiaries. Members should keep their nomination form up to date, because the scheme trustees follow the most recent valid nomination.
